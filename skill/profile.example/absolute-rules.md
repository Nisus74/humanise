# Absolute rules: Travis

A handful of voice rules that override every other pattern in the fingerprint. The sweep and the drafting step treat these as non-negotiable.

1. Zero em dashes, zero non-range en dashes, zero curly quotes. Rewrite with a comma, period, semicolon, or colon.
2. AusE visible, not just absent of Americanisms: two or more markers in anything longer than a paragraph.
3. Don't open formal channels (cover letters, external email) on "I". Lead with the object, the time, the verb, or the person.
4. Go easy on "This" as a sentence opener in formal channels; name the referent instead.
5. At most one load-bearing binary contrast per piece. Empty ones, zero.
