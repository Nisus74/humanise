# Changelog: humanise (engine)

Auditable lineage of engine changes, per `evals/self-harness-loop.md`. Each entry names the target, surface, evidence, and eval result. Newest first.

## 2026-06-25 — voice onboarding and richer fingerprint (Phase 4, standalone scope)

**Target:** voice fidelity is bounded by the corpus, but collecting and annotating samples was laborious and unprompted, and the fingerprint output was stats-heavy and thin on the moves that make a voice distinctive. (The original Phase 4 also planned a dependency-mode voice-fidelity guarantee in the integration contract; that is moot now the skill is standalone, so it was dropped.) **Surface:** added `scripts/corpus-questionnaire.md`, a guided extraction procedure that pulls 5 to 10 samples with a concrete fetch prompt per channel and fills the `SAMPLE_TEMPLATE.md` annotations by asking targeted questions instead of leaving them blank. Enriched the fingerprint output in `scripts/generate-fingerprint.md` and the `profile.template/voice-fingerprint.md` scaffold to require moves, not adjectives: each behavioural section names a concrete habit with a verbatim example and, where it sharpens it, an anti-example, with the stats table demoted to one section. Wired the questionnaire into `commands/init.md`, `profile.template/voice-corpus/README.md`, and the `humanise init` CLI message. **Evidence:** the onboarding-friction and thin-fingerprint gaps named in the review. **Result:** docs and scaffolding only (no detector change); `npm test` green; the new questionnaire dogfoods clean through `humanise detect` (three inline contrasts the checker caught in the first draft were rewritten). **Sync flag:** new `scripts/corpus-questionnaire.md`; `generate-fingerprint.md`, the fingerprint scaffold, `init.md`, the corpus README, and `cli.js` changed; no shared-rule file touched.

## 2026-06-25 — voiceprint-distance measurement loop (Phase 3)

**Target:** the engine measured absence-of-AI against channel-agnostic, AI-derived absolute thresholds but had no automated signal for presence-of-THIS-voice, so the held-out judge (the only real voice gate) was labour-intensive and rarely run. **Surface:** added `voiceprint_features(text)` and `voiceprint_distance(draft, baseline)` to `evals/assertions/writing_checks.py` (a stdlib scalar feature vector reusing existing detector internals: sentence mean and burstiness, copula ratio, specificity, contraction/AusE/stance density, paragraph mean; distance is the mean absolute per-feature z-score against a corpus baseline). `all_checks()` gained an optional `baseline` arg that adds an advisory `voiceprint_distance` block only when supplied, so existing callers and the example evals are unchanged. Added `scripts/build_voiceprint.py` (stdlib) to build a baseline from `profile/voice-corpus/**` into `profile/voiceprint.json` (the soul; never ships, already excluded by `build.mjs`) and to score a draft. Added a `humanise voiceprint <file> | --build` CLI verb mirroring `detect`. Documented the loop: the voiceprint as a tripwire (not a gate) in `evals/indistinguishability.md` and the `self-harness-loop.md` known-limits, the machine-readable twin in `scripts/generate-fingerprint.md` and `commands/fingerprint.md`, and the distance as quantified voice evidence in `SKILL.md` Step 6. **Evidence:** the open-loop gap named in the review. RED-then-GREEN (ImportError before the functions exist, then green); the selftest asserts the vector computes, self-distance is ~0, a thin baseline refuses to flag, and the block is absent without a baseline and advisory-only with one (the Goodhart guard). Smoke-tested end to end: a 3-sample baseline scores a fresh on-voice draft at 1.2 and an anonymous off-voice draft at 3.06, clean discrimination. **Result:** `npm test` green; `npm run check:deps` clean (stdlib Python, Node built-ins, no new deps). **No-tuning design:** advisory (rides the advisory rail, never `failed`), reports a distance not a direction, targets the user's own corpus (no universal number to game), refuses to flag on a thin baseline; the held-out judge stays the real gate. **Sync flag:** `writing_checks.py` changed (engine-shared); new `scripts/build_voiceprint.py` and CLI verb added.

## 2026-06-25 — fully standalone engine (remove dependency mode and all other-skill references)

**Target:** the public engine named the maintainer's private sibling skills (resume, cover-letter, product-management, cto-operating-system) and carried a dependency-mode contract for being called by other skills, both leaks from its private origin. humanise should stand alone: own its workflow end to end, name no other skill, and never run as a style dependency. **Surface:** deleted `references/integration-contract.md` entirely; in `SKILL.md`, replaced the two-roles (standalone/dependency) framing with a single standalone role, removed the dependency-mode reference-list section, and removed the "read as a dependency by the resume and cover-letter skills" sentence from the description; removed the caller-contract clause and its `integration-contract.md` rationale from the top-tier gate row in `evals/self-harness-loop.md`; dropped the "(standalone or dependency mode)" qualifier in `references/memory-loop.md`. Kept legitimate document-genre and dialect references (cover letter as a channel, "CV not resume" in the dialect packs): those name writing genres, not skills. **Evidence:** user directive ("100% standalone"); `CLAUDE.local.md` confirms the resume/cover-letter callers are private and deliberately do not ship with humanise. **Result:** `npm test` green; no dependency-mode or named-skill reference remains in the engine (verified by grep); humanise now runs only its own workflow. **Gate:** removing the caller contract is a top-tier change under `self-harness-loop.md`, authorised by the user's explicit directive; it removes coupling rather than changing drafting behaviour, so no voice regression is possible. **Sync flag:** `SKILL.md`, `self-harness-loop.md`, `memory-loop.md` changed; `integration-contract.md` removed.

## 2026-06-25 — held-in eval coverage expansion

**Target:** thin held-in coverage. Eight checks had an eval lookup but no fixture, and there was no long-form, formal-channel, or docx fixture, so a regression in any of them would pass CI. **Surface:** `evals/assertions/selftest.py` gained coverage for en dash (spaced vs allowed range), spaced-hyphen-as-dash, fragment-colon cluster, academic-register cluster, sentence-length monotony, paragraph-shape uniformity, the binary-contrast density count, the formal `i_this_openers` hard path, the docx curly-quote branch, and a clean >300-word multi-section long-form fixture (exercising the density-scaled contrast budget and the `structural_density` dashboard, and guarding against length false-firing). `evals/evals.json` gained two spec evals: a cover-letter genre eval (formal path) and a docx-medium eval. **Evidence:** the eval-coverage gaps named in the review (checks with a lookup but no fixture; long-form named as where the skill broke worst). **Result:** `selftest.py` green; all 7 evals resolve; the long-form fixture confirms a clean 332-word piece has no hard failure. **Sync flag:** `selftest.py` and `evals.json` are engine files; no shared-rule file changed.

## 2026-06-25 — presence-of-human signals (Phase 2)

**Target:** the engine was almost entirely negative (it strips AI tells) and never measured the *presence* of human texture, so a draft could pass every check and still read clean-but-anonymous. **Surface:** four advisory detectors in `evals/assertions/writing_checks.py`, all riding the existing hard/advisory partition so they report and feed the self-critique but never touch `pass`/`failed`: `specificity_density` (concrete anchors, numbers and proper nouns, per 100 words; flags low on a piece long enough to carry detail), `stance_signal` (presence of opinion/judgement; flags its absence, suppressed for formal/status/board channels), `generic_to_specific` (abstract opener propped up by a borrowed concrete; fuzzy, a re-read nudge), `copula_ratio` (plain to-be/to-have share against copula-avoidance substitutes; also a voiceprint feature). Wired the advisory list into the self-critique pass in `SKILL.md` Step 5 (generation and edit modes) as prompts, not verdicts. Reframed `references/positive-patterns.md` into checkable moves: a framing block mapping the four signals to their moves, plus self-check questions on the specificity, opinion, story-before-lesson, and a new plain-verbs move, keeping the "pick two or three" menu framing so it stays a craft aid not a template. **Evidence:** the presence-of-human gap named in the Phase 2 review; a new `ANON_CLEAN` held-in fixture (clean, varied, but abstract with no specifics or stance) that the signals flag while `_summary.failed` stays empty (the permanent Goodhart guard), plus a counter-fixture (the specific, opinionated `CLEAN_AUS`) that the signals leave alone, and a channel-gating check showing stance suppressed on a board paper. **Result:** `selftest.py` green (6 new assertions); held-out pairwise voice test not regressed by construction (the detectors are report-only and the Step 5 wiring can only add specificity/stance, never remove voice), and not separately runnable in the engine repo, which ships no corpus. **Sync flag:** `writing_checks.py`, `positive-patterns.md` changed (engine-shared); `SKILL.md` Step 5 wording changed.

## 2026-06-25 — detector hardening for 2026 tells (Phase 1)

**Target:** structural and vocabulary tells the pass-two re-read still had to catch by hand, so a dependency-mode caller trusted an incomplete sweep. Six families. **Surface:** in `evals/assertions/writing_checks.py`, (1) extended `META_CANDOUR` with casual-register manufactured candour ("real talk", "not gonna lie", "no sugarcoating", "let me be real", "keeping it real", and kin) so `self_narrated_honesty` (hard, target zero) catches them; (2) added `transition_pileup` (hard at 2+ sentence-initial Moreover/Furthermore/Additionally/Consequently/Notably/Importantly); (3) added a `sentence_reframe` form to `structural_tell_count` for the cross-sentence reframe "This isn't X. It's Y." (reveal must be a copula, so a plain continuation does not false-fire); (4) added `approximation_hedges` (advisory: roughly / sort of / kind of / pretty much / about-a-number); (5) added `dead_verb_density` (advisory: clustering of leverage / unlock / pivot / double down / amplify, narrow list excluding literal-prone verbs); (6) added `balanced_pairs` (advisory: 2+ mirrored subordinator-opener sentences of near-equal length). Registered all in `all_checks()`, added `transition_openers` to the `structural_density` dashboard. Documented each in `references/ai-slop-dictionary.md`, `references/structural-tells.md`, and `references/mechanical-sweep.md`. **Evidence:** detector-coverage gaps named in the Phase 1 review (the pass-two items left to manual re-read); each detector landed RED-then-GREEN with a held-in fixture, including a precision guard (single use does not flag) and, for every advisory signal, a Goodhart guard asserting it appears in `advisory_flagged` and never in `_summary.failed`. **Result:** `selftest.py` green (10 new assertions); CLI `detect` smoke-tested end-to-end; held-out pairwise voice test not yet re-run (the three new advisory signals cannot lower judge-confusion, being report-only). **Sync flag:** `writing_checks.py`, `ai-slop-dictionary.md`, and `structural-tells.md` changed; re-sync into the maintainer's private working skill.

## 2026-06-24 — initial open-source release

Engine extracted from a personal writing skill into a forkable body/soul structure. The engine (`SKILL.md`, `references/`, `evals/`, `scripts/`) is universal; the profile (soul, fingerprint, corpus, dialect, absolute rules) is per-user. Ships a blank `profile.template/`, a scrubbed `profile.example/`, the fingerprint generator, the held-in `selftest.py`, dialect packs (en-AU, en-US, en-GB), and the Self-Harness self-improvement loop as the contribution gate. Defines two agents (`agents/adversarial-reviewer.md`, `agents/indistinguishability-judge.md`) and wires them into the verification steps and the self-harness gate. selftest green on the generic example evals.
