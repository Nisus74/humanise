# Voice fingerprint: <your name>

The distilled model of how you decide and write, read selectively for the current channel. **Don't
write it by hand.** Generate it from your evidence:

1. Start with one real sample, then grow towards 5 to 10 across the channels you use. Save any
   draft-to-final pairs and negative examples too.
2. Run the generator in `scripts/generate-fingerprint.md`.
3. Paste the result here, then refine the parts that don't ring true.

Until you do, the skill leans on your `soul.md` and `absolute-rules.md`, and it will tell you it's drafting without a fingerprint rather than pretending. The corpus is the highest-leverage thing you can add; everything else is rules.

A good fingerprint starts with selection, judgement, relationship and structure, then covers cadence
and diction. Give every observation a scope, evidence and confidence label. Write each as a decision or
move, not an adjective. “Opens investor updates on the period's hardest number” beats “clear and
direct”. Keep one-off observations provisional.
