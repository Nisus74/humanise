# Voice fingerprint: <your name>

The distilled model of *how* you write, read on every draft. **Don't write it by hand.** Generate it from your samples:

1. Put 5 to 10 real samples of your writing into `voice-corpus/` (see its README).
2. Run the generator in `scripts/generate-fingerprint.md`.
3. Paste the result here, then refine the parts that don't ring true.

Until you do, the skill leans on your `soul.md` and `absolute-rules.md`, and it will tell you it's drafting without a fingerprint rather than pretending. The corpus is the highest-leverage thing you can add; everything else is rules.

A good fingerprint covers: opening and closing moves, recurring phrasings, sentence habits (length variation, fragments, active voice), how you handle disagreement, humour, the things you never do, channel-specific adjustments, and a short table of measurable tripwires. See `references/` and the generator for the full shape.
