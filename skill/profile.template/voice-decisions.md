# Voice decisions

Record what the writer chooses, not only how their sentences look. Each observation needs a confidence
label and evidence.

## Confirmed

<Repeated in three independent examples or explicitly confirmed by the writer.>

## Supported

<Seen twice, or seen once and confirmed by the writer.>

## Provisional

<Seen once or inferred during calibration. Keep these out of absolute rules.>

Use this shape:

- **Decision:** <what the writer chooses or notices>
- **Scope:** <channel, relationship and purpose>
- **Evidence:** <sample or edit pair>
- **Confidence:** confirmed | supported | provisional
- **Boundary:** <where this should not generalise>
